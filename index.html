About data

iAWE supplied the washer session and the refrigerator and AC readings for 28 June 2013 in New Delhi. The dishwasher, dryer, their start-time forecasts and the tariff use simulated inputs. The washer forecast uses earlier iAWE sessions. Missing seconds contribute zero to the slot-average power estimates.
